# Gulf women's dance in the thobe al-nashal (ثوب النشل): no usable footage

**Status: no reference pack was made.** No keyframes, clips or timing figures exist for this dance. Between
2026-10-04 and 2026-10-05 I searched YouTube in English and Arabic and screened about 45 candidates on contact
sheets (plus 20 to 25 second samples from six long broadcasts). None of them is a stage or troupe performance of
women dancing in a thobe al-nashal with the head covered. The rules for women's scenes were: stage, TV or
heritage-troupe performances only, head covered, nothing that looks covertly filmed at a private or women-only
event. Everything below is what the sampled frames show; the per-video detail and the exact tile times are in
`analysis/LOG.md` (Drive, see below).

## What was searched

About 70 queries with `yt-dlp ytsearch`, in Arabic (ثوب النشل, رقصة ثوب النشل, فن خماري, فن سامري, الليوا, النقازي, فرقة نسائية, فرقة الفنون الشعبية النسائية, with
Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Janadriyah, Al-Qurain, Katara, Sheikh Zayed festival) and English (Gulf
women folk dance thobe nashal troupe stage, Khammari, Samri, Al Naashat, and the same with each country). Four
WebSearch queries returned nothing relevant. I also listed whole channels and searched them by title: Bahrain TV Zaman (273
videos), Kuwait's National Council for Culture, Arts and Letters (1,187), Bahrain TV (3,000 newest) and a Kuwait TV archive
channel (6,000 newest). The dance is named in titles in three ways: "ثوب النشل" (the gown, in a song and a clip title),
"فن خماري" (Khammari) and "فن سامري" (Samri). No source calls a stage item "the thobe al-nashal dance", so I have no
source-given name for it.

## The two closest candidates (head covered, TV production): not built, left for the project owner to decide

Neither shows a thobe al-nashal that I can identify, and neither is the dance in the brief, so I did not build a pack.

1. **`LLv5UhVrd9E`, "فن خماري - شمة بنت حمود" (Khammari), Bahrain TV Zaman, 9:17, 720p copy on machine A.** A Bahrain TV
   recording in a courtyard set. Men in white hold frame drums and a rope-laced barrel drum in a ring. A row of
   women stands at a microphone in printed and pastel gowns with gold braid at the neckline, a cloth worn over the head.
   One woman in a blue floral-print gown, the same cloth over her head, moves among the men with her head bowed toward
   them (about 2:00 to 2:49 and 4:00 to 4:45). Wide shots are a few seconds long; most of the video is close cuts.
   The women mostly sing; the dancing is led by the men. Gowns are printed cotton.
2. **`2purQzFN9Tg` (8:52), `RLqN9QKTKwQ` (8:56), `ptI229veiXE` (4:39), `p0J3FGVNcro` (4:38), "لوحات شعبية - شمة بنت حمود", Bahrain TV Zaman.**
   The same staged village-courtyard set in all four. A soloist in a red gown with gold braid and a gold-edged hood
   sings in close shots; wide shots show about a dozen women, shawls over their heads, walking in a ring with long
   wooden poles and wooden mortars, some pounding. A grain-pounding work-song scene, not a dance in a nashal gown.

## Not covered

- **The thobe al-nashal itself.** The only close views of gold-thread embroidery I found are in a children's song video
  (`9Y6oFlwpVhg`, 1:36) and in the uncovered-head Kuwaiti and Bahraini studio recordings; none of these was usable.
- **Not screened, so not ruled out:** the follow-up list in `LOG.md` section 3 (a Kuwaiti "women's group" concert, Qatari
  and Saudi troupe concerts, Bahrain TV heritage-village programmes, Qatar TV "Folk Arts" episodes 2, 3, 5 to 8, an Omani
  women's-arts archive, Kuwait TV "From Our Heritage" programmes).
- **Sampling limits.** Sheets used steps of 3 to 40 s and the head-covering calls were made on 320 px tiles; for Diriyah,
  Muradat, Kuwait Heritage and Kuwait TV I re-checked a full 640x360 frame. A short women's item between tiles, or in an
  unscreened part of a long programme, could have been missed.
